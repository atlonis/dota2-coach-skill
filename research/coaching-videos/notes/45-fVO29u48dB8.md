## Core idea

The coach argues against the common advice for climbing on carry:
- stand the lane passively;
- hide in the jungle;
- skip fights;
- farm 500 creeps and win alone.

He plays the opposite way:
- aggressive in lane, punishing every enemy mistake;
- joining nearby fights;
- pushing waves deep;
- farming the enemy's camps before they can ("scorched earth");
- fighting on his item's cooldown, and farming when it is down.

This is a deliberate counterpoint to notes 23 and 31, where coaches put farm first. Read together, the difference is less about farm versus fights than about where the carry farms and when he joins.
