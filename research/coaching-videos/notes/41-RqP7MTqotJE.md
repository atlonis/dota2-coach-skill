## Core idea

Good micro starts with a comfortable key setup. Then two modifiers do most of the work: one sends a command to a single highlighted unit, and the other queues commands. With them you set up summons and illusions once and go back to your own hero. The guide ends with uses of extra units:
- blocking or stacking camps;
- body-blocking;
- scouting;
- farming at a safe distance;
- dragging waves.
