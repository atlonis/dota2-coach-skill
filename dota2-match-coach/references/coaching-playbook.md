# Coaching playbook

Coaching opinion from 50 Dota 2 coaching videos; note numbers are maintainer provenance (`research/coaching-videos/notes/`), not citations for the player. Nothing here is a current-patch fact, a peer norm or match evidence.

Use it after the match facts are set: turn one supported finding into labelled advice ("Совет тренера:" / "Coach's advice:") with its condition, plus one drill below. A signal *can point to* a mistake; say in one clause what it cannot show. Never present a rule of thumb as measured in this match or as what this rank does.

## By position

### 1 · Carry

**Priorities:** farm fast and don't die (23, 47); lane items and regen for the lane's problem; lanes before camps (14); fights from the farming route, not instead of it (02); turn the key item into towers and Roshan (12).

- *Weak lane farm.* Signal: last hits and denies at 10:00 below the lane opponents', or a low last-hit percentile. Can't show: wave position, creep aggro, who zoned whom.
- *Late key item.* Signal: a major-item timing later than the enemy carry's; a low GPM percentile. Can't show: where he farmed, idle time.
- *Isolated midgame deaths.* Signal: deaths after the lane outside recorded fights, long time dead, large gold lost. Can't show: vision, or what he was doing there.
- *Fighting before the item.* Signal: several deaths in recorded fights before the major-item timing. Can't show: who started the fight.

### 2 · Mid

**Priorities:** hit the enemy mid while last hitting (22, 34); push the wave before every rune, gank or base trip (12, 46); fast ganks with a reason (15); after the enemy tower falls, farm behind it (15); a TP and your own observer (26).

- *Lost mid.* Signal: last hits, denies, XP or net worth at 10:00 behind the enemy mid. Can't show: runes, harass, wave state.
- *Kills without farm.* Signal: many kills but a low last-hit or GPM percentile. Can't show: waves lost while roaming.
- *Lane deaths to ganks.* Signal: deaths before 10:00 to two or more heroes, or to a tower. Can't show: vision, whether the wave was pushed.
- *Passive after a won lane.* Signal: ahead at 10:00, yet major items no earlier than the enemy cores'. Can't show: where he farmed.

### 3 · Offlane

**Priorities:** a core, so keep net worth (25); resource trades, XP range when alone (25); after a lost lane, push, farm, don't die before the survival item (48); initiate with the team, around the enemy's save (05); buy for this game's main problem (25).

- *Forcing lane fights.* Signal: repeated deaths before 10:00 to the lane opponents. Can't show: health, mana, creep count.
- *No recovery after a lost lane.* Signal: behind at 10:00, then more deaths before the survival item. Can't show: where he farmed.
- *Low farm after the lane.* Signal: a low GPM or last-hit percentile; a net-worth gap to the enemy offlaner. Can't show: wasted teleports (25).
- *Initiating alone.* Signal: deaths in recorded fights with few allies involved. Can't show: who jumped first, enemy cooldowns.

### 4 · Soft support

**Priorities:** lane pressure that makes the enemy core miss creeps (38); 2v1 trades on creep count and power spikes (38, 44); rotations with a timing and purpose (09); after the lane, join the strongest ally or make space (04, 44).

- *Passive lane.* Signal: low hero damage; the own core behind the enemy core at 10:00. Can't show: harass, pulls, zoning.
- *Roams that cost the lane.* Signal: the roaming flag, the lane ally behind at 10:00, few kills or assists. Can't show: what the roam forced elsewhere.
- *Dying early or alone.* Signal: deaths in recorded fights, or outside fights to several heroes. Can't show: held spells, vision (10).
- *Items the team already has.* Signal: final items against the allies'. The fit is a judgement (16, 44).

### 5 · Hard support

**Priorities:** the core's lane goals: levels, an item, a good wave (50); own regen, wards, sentries, a smoke (11, 37); the next ward planned ahead (19); alive long enough to cast everything (50); saves on the focused ally (16).

- *Thin vision.* Signal: few observer or sentry placements, long gaps between them. Can't show: placement quality, dewards, enemy wards.
- *Core's lane lost.* Signal: the lane ally behind at 10:00. Can't show: pulls, blocks; the core shares that result.
- *Dying first or alone.* Signal: deaths in fights, or outside fights to several heroes. Can't show: positioning, cooldowns.
- *Save items late or missing.* Signal: purchase timings and final items against the enemy lineup. The fit is a judgement (16).

## Cross-position principles

- **Deaths.** How you die matters more than how often: lane deaths to ganks are recoverable; overextended deaths after the lane break games. For every role, late deaths outside big fights (long respawn, big gold loss, the team defending four against five) are usually the most expensive ones.
- **Teleport.** Always carry one; don't spend it on comfort or straight after respawn; never teleport away from a forming fight.
- **Map reading.** Fear only enemies who can reach you; treat unseen ones as near; the search starts where you last showed.
- **Fight reasons.** An objective, a ready key item or spell, numbers. With none, and the fight isn't quick, farm.
- **Conversion.** After a won fight: waves, then a tower or Roshan, or reset. Don't dive the second tower or overchase.
- **Itemization.** Buy for this game's problem; cores take survivability before damage.
- **Waves.** Push before leaving a lane; lanes before camps. Waves aren't recorded, so this is always advice, never a finding.
- **Vision.** Ward for a fight, a farming area or a tower; cores can buy their own.
- **Late game.** Don't siege on the Aegis alone; pick someone off first; keep buyback gold.

**Disagreements:** farm-first carry (23, 47) versus early aggression (22, 45); the creep block (26 versus 34); farming behind a lost tower (28) versus deep in enemy space (45); Roshan for a slow carry (31). Pick a side only when rank, role, lane result or team economy supports it; otherwise give the shared ground: push lanes, don't die, farm enemy space when strong.

## Next-game drills

**Name** (finding it fits) — trigger → action. *Self-check.* No replay needed.

1. **Name the death** (repeated deaths) — each death → one reason before respawn: caught alone, chased, dove, lost fight, no TP. *Tally deaths by reason.*
2. **Count before you go** (deaths outside big fights, isolated deaths) — before a far wave, camp, ward spot or solo gank → count enemies on the minimap; three or more missing → stay nearer your team or go with a core. *Count deaths on the enemy half of the map with no ally on your screen.*
3. **TP discipline** (late to fights, deaths after returning to lane) — respawn or shop visit → check the TP slot, name where it is needed next, walk if you can. *Count TPs used straight from the fountain.*
4. **TP into a live fight** (deaths soon after joining fights) — before teleporting in → check the target is alive, count enemies there, cancel if it is over. *Count TPs that ended in your death within 15 seconds.*
5. **The wave question** (low lane last hits against the lane opponent) — every wave before 10:00 → ask "what can kill me here alone?"; nothing → take the wave. *At 10:00, write your last hits and your opponent's from the scoreboard.*
6. **Push before you move** (kills without farm, early towers lost) — before a rune, gank or base trip → clear the wave. *Count moves made with the wave unpushed.*
7. **Name the fight's reason** (deaths in fights, chain deaths) — before joining → name one reason; none and not quick → farm. *Count fights joined without a reason, and deaths in them.*
8. **Key item first** (late key item) — until it is built → be at the next free wave; join only fights on the way. *Write the minute it finished; count fights joined before it.*
9. **Reset after a win** (lead lost after won fights) — enemies dead → waves, then a tower or Roshan if close, then refill. *Count won fights followed by an objective.*
10. **Spend the gold** (large gold lost on deaths) — gold for a component → buy or queue it. *Count deaths while holding gold for a component.*
11. **Ward stock** (few wards, support or core) — every base or courier trip → keep an observer and a sentry on you; plan the next ward while walking. *Count wards placed, from the post-game screen.*
12. **Four lane questions** (passive support lane) — in lane → where do waves meet, is a 2v1 on, who has more creeps, did a spike just land. *Count trades you started that your core joined.*
13. **Tower check** (deaths to towers) — before hitting or chasing under an enemy tower → go only if most enemies show elsewhere. *Count deaths under enemy towers.*
14. **Ward and leave** (support deaths while warding) — before a ward on the enemy half → count missing enemies, place it and walk back at once; with Dust or sentries on the enemy side, don't trust invisibility. *Count deaths within a minute of placing a ward.*

## Rules of thumb

One coach's figure at one rank, not a peer statistic. Never quote it as a norm the player failed; use it only as "Совет тренера" with its condition. A target needs a ready peer comparison or the player's own history.

- Farming carry at ~5.5K: about 80 last hits by 10–11 minutes, 350 by 25 (23).
- Net worth at 10 minutes: 5K+ for a carry with lane kills (28); 4K+ for a mid at ~5K (22).
- Cores at 10–12K net worth by 20 minutes (49); a ~10K support at about 50 last hits by 20 (44).
- In lane, health above 80% and mana above 50% (49).
- Fewer than three deaths a game while holding an unused spell (10).
- Always carry a TP (14, 22, 46).
- Before 25–30 minutes a carry plays away from the team about 85:15 (02).

Tone:

- Talk like a teammate who coaches: short sentences, verb first. «Пушь волну перед руной», not «рекомендуется осуществлять пуш». «Ты» only when the user said the match is theirs; otherwise «игрок на <Hero>».
- No канцелярит: «осуществить», «является», «данный», «в рамках».
- No filler: «в целом», «следует отметить», «важно понимать».
- Slang yes; insults for the player or teammates no.
- Hero and item names as the artifact gives them; no invented nicknames.
